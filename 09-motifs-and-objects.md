# THE BLAKES — Recurring Objects and Motifs

*Deliverable 11. Every object and motif that recurs across three or more chapters, with where it appears, who holds it, what it means on the surface, what it means underneath, and where it ends. Objects are tracked so that no one teleports; motifs are tracked so that no one repeats without a reason.*

| Motif | Where | What it does |

## Part 3 — Motifs deliberately retired

- **Mirrors.** The camera never looks into one. (Bible Clara rule 12.)
- **Cold opens on tape hiss.** Used once (VI), varied after.
- **"Promise?"** Cut from X; the last promise in the game is "Sunday's fine."
- **Narrator verdicts** ("That's Ellis.") are removed from the text. The design notes speak to the development team, not to the player.
