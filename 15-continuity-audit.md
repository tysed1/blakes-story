# THE BLAKES — Continuity Audit

*Deliverable 17 (phase 13). This is the state of the canon after the V6 and V7 repairs: what was checked, how, what was wrong, and what it is now. The full critic reports are in `00-process/critic-reports/`: A and B cover I–VI, and C and D cover the complete draft. The lead's decisions are in `00-process/V4-red-team.md` and `00-process/V6-V7-audits.md`.*

## 1. How it was checked

- **Chapters I–VI.** Two independent critics (A: character and dialogue; B: continuity and systems) read them against the bible. Their findings became work orders R1 and R2, which were carried out and logged (`repair-log-R1.md`, `repair-log-R2.md`).
- **Chapters VII–Epilogue.** Critic C read them in full against the bible, the worklog, the V4 plan and the macrostructure. C also checked I–VI wherever a later chapter cites them.
- **Dates.** A script checked every explicit weekday-plus-date in VII–Epilogue: **190 of 190** were correct. The lead re-checked every date in the master timeline below after the V7 changes.
- **Counts.** Rule-bound words and phrases were counted with grep and checked by hand (§3).
- **Period facts.** These were checked against the research notes, with additions verified in `13-historical-authenticity.md`.
- **Real-city leaks.** A grep across all chapters for Atlanta, Nashville, Tennessee, Peachtree, Braves, Falcons, Opry, Ryman, Music Row, Vanderbilt, Emory, Buckhead, Juniper and Midtown found none for the two cities. "Midtown" appears only for New York.

## 4. Geography (final)

- **Tolliver Road** is a through road. It runs south past the Tolliver farm, past the Hensley house, and 0.3 mi on round the Bend (the white oak with the scar), then climbs back to the Tanner Valley road east of Roy's. It's the back way home when the low-water bridge on the South Fork road floods, which is why Ellis took it in the rain on April 12, 1973. The county sawhorse (ROAD WORK — LOCAL TRAFFIC) is there only in Chapter I; after that Ellis simply won't turn onto it. *(V6 repair: the chapters had put the Bend beyond the farm, heading away from town.)*
- **The chalkboard** hangs by Marlon's front door. On Aug 26, 1976, Marlon carries it out to the back step for the bus; by the funeral it's back by the door; by 1996 it's framed behind the bar.
- **Glen Arbor Raceway**, Schuyler County, NY. The stage faces east.
  - Stage left is the performer's left, facing the crowd.
  - The riser is upstage center; Riley stands at its stage-left corner for song endings.
  - The film crew works from the stage-right wing.
  - The gap (a yard wide, twelve feet down) is between the deck and the stage-left PA wing.
  - Facing upstage from the stage-left lip, Riley is ahead of him and to his left, the light comes from past her shoulder, and the gap is on his right.
- **After the fall.** The ambulance goes Route 14 south to the hospital in Elmira, forty minutes. The way home is Route 17 east to Binghamton, then I-81 south through Pennsylvania, Maryland and Virginia (the truck stop near Wytheville), then into Georgia.
