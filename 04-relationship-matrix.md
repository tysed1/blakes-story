# Relationship Arc Matrix

*Deliverable 6. A great ensemble is a web, not spokes around a protagonist. Every pair below has a history that exists whether or not Ellis is in the room.*

Fields: **Start** (starting assumption) · **Friction** · **Bond** (bonding event) · **Joke** (private joke) · **Betrayal** (first betrayal) · **Disagreement** (deepest) · **Admires** (secretly) · **Misreads** · **Late** (late-game state) · **Last** (final memory, the thing the survivor keeps)

---

## The band

### Ellis ↔ Riley
- **Start:** She's a college girl slumming at Marlon's. / He's a mountain kid with one good song.
- **Friction:** "Thinking about taking it?" "Seeing if it'd be worth the felony." Friction as flirtation; class underneath (her "Some of us have futures," Ch II).
- **Bond:** The harmony on "No Name" (Ch I): she sings the part Grace used to sing, and he says "Again." Then the quad (II): Grace's name.
- **Joke:** "That's usually a yes from musicians." / "Maybe." Also "Again."
- **Betrayal:** Hers first: she tells Cal about Clara in Ch VII because she's scared. His: the hotel-bar kiss she reads about in a magazine (VIII), and, worse, lying about the pills (IX).
- **Disagreement:** Whether altered perception reveals truth. She starts believing it does; he ends up using it to try to reach Grace; she ends up saying it only turns things up.
- **Admires:** He admires that she chose her own name and her own life. She admires that he *sees*, specifically, the thing nobody else noticed.
- **Misreads:** She thinks she has special access to him; she has the access he permits. He thinks she'd leave if she knew about the car.
- **Late:** Not together; closer than ever. On the dock (IX): "After, I'm going home for a while." "Good."
- **Last:** Her hand out on the stage. For her, afterward: the notebooks, and poems addressed to "M."

### Ellis ↔ Cal
- **Start:** "Friendly." / "Working." Each thinks the other is exactly what he looks like.
- **Friction:** Criticism. "Your second verse needs work." "Nobody asked you." "You played it in front of me."
- **Bond:** Cal follows Ellis's wrong chord in the first set (I); Ellis sees it. Cal rearranges the money so Ellis can pay later (II). Ellis sees that too.
- **Joke:** "Think in E minor." / "That's just his face." / "It was a turn."
- **Betrayal:** Ellis outs Cal in front of Dean and Tully: "At least mine isn't somebody I keep in a hotel room" (VIII).
- **Disagreement:** Structure versus chaos; whether pain makes art important ("Pain doesn't automatically make everything you do important." / "And being understandable doesn't make it good.").
- **Admires:** Cal admires Ellis's nerve. Ellis admires Cal's certainty and, secretly, Cal's father.
- **Misreads:** Cal thinks Ellis wants to be excused. Ellis thinks Cal wants to be in charge.
- **Late:** Mended at a repair bench (IX); fishing; the grandfather story; "You are the point." (X)
- **Last:** For Cal: the list and the ledger margin. "He wasn't so bad."

### Ellis ↔ Dean
- **Start:** "Who are you?" "Drummer." "For what?" "Whatever this is."
- **Friction:** Class. Dean has a pool; Ellis has a bald tire. The Holloway Christmas (V), where Dean's father is kind to Ellis and Dean hates it.
- **Bond:** "Need anything?" / "No." / "Didn't say money." (II). Dean knows when not to make something serious. The hood of the van in the rain: "being forty" (V).
- **Joke:** The four dollars. "That's a tomorrow problem." "Demand."
- **Betrayal:** Dean gives Ellis truck-stop speed for a night drive (VII), and then cocaine on the arena tour (VIII), without thinking of it as anything.
- **Disagreement:** Whether you should ever stop.
- **Admires:** Dean admires that Ellis is real in a way nobody in Belle Grove is. Ellis admires that Dean is happy, and that his father is a good man.
- **Misreads:** Dean thinks Ellis's darkness is authenticity. Ellis thinks Dean's lightness is shallowness.
- **Late:** Reversed: sober Dean saying "We can cancel," Ellis refusing.
- **Last:** The Polaroid in Dean's shirt pocket. "He was funny. Nobody writes that."

### Riley ↔ Cal
- **Start:** She's a hobbyist. / He's a scold.
- **Friction:** The farmhouse (III): "You give Ellis whatever he took?" / "He chose." / "Neither do you [know him]."
- **Bond:** The studio (III–IV): both hear the same wrong note. She sees him with Theo (VI) and says nothing to anyone for a year.
- **Joke:** Lists versus crosswords. "Both can be true." / "No."
- **Betrayal:** Neither betrays the other; Cal blames her for the mushrooms (VI) in a single sentence he takes back the next day.
- **Disagreement:** Whether you can protect someone from themselves.
- **Admires:** He admires her ear. She admires his steadiness and that he's at peace with himself.
- **Misreads:** He thinks she's naive. She thinks he's unfeeling.
- **Late:** The two adults in the kitchen with Wayne (VIII). In the epilogue, the two who fight the label for the footage and the album.
- **Last:** Riley and Cal in 1996 in his studio, releasing *Who Are You?* with the last verse left empty.

### Riley ↔ Dean
- **Start:** "Oh Christ." / "Riley!" / "Why do you know my name?"
- **Friction:** He mocks school; she mocks him ("You are literally the worst advertisement for higher education I've ever seen").
- **Bond:** The parties. They understand each other's rebellion: she wants experiences that make her think, he wants experiences that stop him thinking, and at twenty they look identical from outside.
- **Joke:** "Professor." Also the five-dollar bet on the kiss.
- **Betrayal:** None, which is the point: the one uncomplicated friendship in the band.
- **Disagreement:** The ERA, loudly, in the van.
- **Admires:** He admires her brain. She admires that he's never once been embarrassed.
- **Misreads:** She thinks his using is the same as hers. It isn't.
- **Late:** She tells him he's using more effectively than Cal ever does, because she isn't judging (VIII).
- **Last:** Dean, sober, driving her home from the funeral in her own car because she can't.

### Cal ↔ Dean
- **Start:** "No." / "You don't even know what I want." / "Money." / (door opens)
- **Friction:** Time, tempo and cocaine. "Then stop acting like him."
- **Bond:** "Kick with me. You're playing at me." (II) The rhythm section locks and Dean says "Do that again," without a joke.
- **Joke:** "Dad." / "Eleven!" / "TEN." / the ledger.
- **Betrayal:** Dean hires the Starlite's cook's cousin as a roadie without telling Cal, twice; the punch (V) is the real one.
- **Disagreement:** Whether responsibility protects you.
- **Admires:** Dean admires that Cal is exactly who he is. Cal admires that Dean can make any room love him.
- **Misreads:** Dean hears his father in Cal. Cal thinks Dean doesn't know he's in trouble; Dean knows exactly.
- **Late:** Cal sits up with Dean for three nights (IX) reading *The Dispossessed* aloud in a monotone.
- **Last:** The two of them loading out the festival stage the next day because somebody has to.

## The Blakes family

### Ellis ↔ Wayne
- **Start:** Two men in a house with a closed door.
- **Friction:** Every conversation; "cars after the fact"; "You got to keep yours"; "I already buried one child"; "She know about Grace?"
- **Bond:** Wordless and practical: the wax-paper lunch (I), half a tire (II), a mountain in the dark (III), a car heater (V), forty miles an hour home from the ballpark (V), peanuts on a tailgate (IX).
- **Joke:** "Hm." Georgia football on the radio in separate rooms. Tater, fed twice.
- **Betrayal:** Wayne knew there was another truck and let Ellis believe it was his fault.
- **Disagreement:** What Grace would want.
- **Admires:** Wayne admires Ellis's work (in the studio, VI). Ellis admires that Wayne kept going to work every single day.
- **Misreads:** Each thinks the other blames him. Both are right, and both are wrong.
- **Late:** "You want me there?" / "Yeah."
- **Last:** For Wayne: the drive home; the dog getting thin; the headstone; the door he won't open for a documentary crew.

### Ellis ↔ Clara
- **Start:** Old friends. She's "from before."
- **Friction:** Gentle teasing, then possessiveness as others take her place (the harmony, the audience).
- **Bond:** Every night he couldn't be alone. "You're allowed to have something good."
- **Joke:** "Liar." "Second one was better."
- **Betrayal:** "Don't tell them." She keeps him from help to keep herself.
- **Disagreement:** Whether he's allowed to leave.
- **Admires:** He admires everything about her because he made her out of what he loved.
- **Misreads:** He thinks she's someone he knows. She is.
- **Late:** "I couldn't be her."
- **Last:** Nothing left to hold.

### Ellis ↔ Grace
- **Start:** Brother and sister, three years apart, their mother gone.
- **Friction:** She's in his room, on his records, in his cigarettes, in his car.
- **Bond:** Harmony on the Louvin Brothers; WGRC; "You're my brother."
- **Joke:** "Grace Made Me Late." The Turnip. Loretta.
- **Betrayal:** "When I go, I'm not coming back for you."
- **Disagreement:** Leaving.
- **Admires:** She admired that he was going to get out. He admired that she stood up to Daddy.
- **Misreads:** He thinks she died hating him. She died teasing him.
- **Last:** "It's okay. Go on."

## Across the web

### Wayne ↔ Riley
- **Start:** "That the college girl?" / "Her name's Riley." / "Fine. Riley."
- **Bond:** The kitchen on Cold Branch Road (VIII): she drives up alone to ask him about his son. He tells her about Raymond, Milledgeville and Grace singing harmony, things he has never told Ellis. "Does she have a name?" "Clara." He sits down. "That was my mama's name."
- **Late:** The hospital corridor in New York. "He was good. Wasn't he." "Yes." "I never told him." "He knew." "No. He didn't."
- **Last:** Wayne giving her the notebooks. "Don't put all of it in a book." "I won't."

### Wayne ↔ Cal
- **Start:** "Smart kid." / "He's an asshole." / "Usually goes together."
- **Bond:** Cal fixes the radio in Wayne's truck (VII) without being asked; Wayne notices it's been done and says nothing, which Cal understands perfectly.
- **Late:** The phone call (VIII): two practical men deciding what to do about someone they both love and can't say so.
- **Last:** Cal returning Ellis's guitar case to Cold Branch Road. They stand in the yard. "You fix that radio?" "Yes sir." "Hm."

### Wayne ↔ Dean
- **Start:** Mutual incomprehension. ("Would've hated Dean.")
- **Late:** Dean, sober, months after, on Wayne's porch. "You look better." "I am." "Good. Somebody ought to be."

### Ellis ↔ Roy
- **Start:** Boss and a boy he's watching out for.
- **Bond:** The ridge and the radio (III). "Guess you better write another one."
- **Joke:** "Allegedly." "You'd get more for the truck." The two transmissions.
- **Late:** "Just don't become an asshole." "Too late." "Fair." Danny, mentioned once.
- **Last:** Roy drives Wayne to New York and sleeps in the passenger seat all the way home.

### Ellis ↔ Marlon
- **Start:** A bar owner who charges him for beers.
- **Secret:** Marlon invited him to play on April 12, 1973.
- **Last:** The chalkboard behind the bar, never erased. Marlon to the 1996 camera: "Second verse was always better."

### Ellis ↔ Patty Holloway
- **Start:** A fifteen-year-old in a Christmas crowd who isn't impressed.
- **Bond:** He talks to her like a coworker. He's gentler with her than Dean has ever seen him with anyone. She writes him a letter about "Borrowed Stone" (VII); he writes back.
- **Last:** The letter framed in her college dorm.

### Ellis ↔ Tully
- **Bond:** Two men who don't talk about what happened to them, carrying amplifiers.
- **Last:** Tully is the first one down into the gap.

### Ellis ↔ Dex Lundgren
- **Start:** Dex is funny, smart, reads poetry; Ellis likes him.
- **Betrayal:** A notebook page, pocketed on the bus (VIII); the poems in print (IX).
- **Last:** "I made him up. Some of him. He let me."

### Cal ↔ Walt Mercer
- **Start:** "Gig tomorrow?" / "Supposed to." / "Pays?" / "Then it's work."
- **Bond:** Talking while facing an engine or a chassis, never each other.
- **Late:** Walt at a show at the Paramount: "Bass was loud." (That's pride.)
- **Last:** At the funeral, Walt meets Theo. "You're the sound man." "Yes sir." "He says you're good." That's all, and it's everything.

### Cal ↔ Theo
- **Start:** The Lantern (IV). "You ever want work in Laurel City, call."
- **Bond:** Sunday mornings; a reel-to-reel only Cal can fix.
- **Late:** Theo sees the fall from the mixing tower and walks eighty yards through the crowd without remembering doing it.
- **Last:** In 1996, glimpsed in the doorway of Cal's studio, unexplained.

### Riley ↔ Joan Riley
- **Start:** "Maggie." A mother who says "You're very lucky" and means everything by it.
- **Bond:** Voice-leading at the piano. "Every voice has somewhere it wants to go."
- **Friction:** Oberlin, 1949, which Riley only learns about in Ch VII.
- **Late:** Joan hears "Sunday Clothes" on the radio sung by a boy she fed at Thanksgiving, and calls her daughter, and doesn't say anything about the singer.
- **Last:** Joan at the funeral, the only person who goes to the piano in the church afterward and plays a shape-note tune she learned from Ellis.

### Riley ↔ Hannah
- **Start:** Roommates who argue about efficiency.
- **Drift:** The band takes Riley; Hannah graduates and teaches fourth grade.
- **Last:** Hannah at the funeral, holding Riley's coat, knowing where everything is.

### Riley ↔ Landry
- **Start:** "Would you like to join us?"
- **Bond:** He never tells her to quit. "You're allowed more than one ambition."
- **Last:** A three-line note after the funeral.

### Dean ↔ Richard Holloway
- **Start:** "At some point you've got to decide what you're doing with your life."
- **Bond:** The contract (VI): watching his father be brilliant on his behalf.
- **Betrayal:** Cutting him off (VII). From Dean's side: "The invoice."
- **Late:** "I'm terrified you will [regret it]."
- **Last:** Richard driving to upstate New York the night of the fall and finding his son in a motel parking lot. "Dad." That's where they start over.

### Dean ↔ Lynette
- **Start:** "Coffee?" "Whatever keeps me up." "Everything keeps you up, honey."
- **Late:** She hangs up on him (VIII); he comes back sober (IX).
- **Last:** Bobby's drawing of a drum kit taped inside Dean's bass drum.

### Dean ↔ Tully
- **Bond:** Saigon night: Dean sits beside him in a motel parking lot for an hour and says nothing, the only time in the game Dean is silent.

### Riley ↔ Nina Sorensen
- **Bond:** The first critic to write about Riley as a musician (VIII).
- **Last:** Nina's short column after the death: "He was nineteen."

### Wayne ↔ Lorraine
- **Last:** An envelope handed back unopened at a graveside. "Wayne." "Lorraine."

### Wayne ↔ Roy
- **Start:** Old friends who stopped talking about anything that mattered in 1968.
- **Late:** Two old men driving nine hundred miles in an F-100 to watch a boy play.
- **Last:** The drive home. Roy asleep. Wayne driving.

### The band ↔ Eddie Farris
- **Start:** "You got thirty minutes. Don't tune forever."
- **Late:** "Your call."
- **Last:** Eddie paying for the funeral flowers out of his own fifteen percent and never mentioning it.

### The band ↔ Frank Dalton
- **Last:** Frank refusing to mix the label's *Last Light* and, in 1996, mixing *Who Are You?* for free.
