## 5. Pacing and clarity

- **The only-guidance idea is barely taught in Chapter I.** Clara helps in M1 and M9 only. M2, Ellis's long daytime mission, has none, and M3–M7 are no-help POVs by design, so about 3.5 hours pass between her lines. Many players won't register her as the game's guidance system until II. Add one more practical line on the M1 drive and a money line at the M9 Starlite stop.
- **No mission specifies its HUD objective string.** §11.8 allows one plain objective, and in the Riley, Cal and Dean segments it is the player's only guidance. Add an **Objective:** field to every mission header. This is a production blocker.
- **IV M4 (72 min) and M8 (75 min) are each two missions.** Split M4 after the ice machine and M8 at "Laurel City after midnight". Keep the Holler House ("Nothing special happens") under eight minutes.
- **III M6 puts too many clues together four hours before the midpoint.** "talking to yourself", the unbroken frost, "Riley couldn't see you", then M8's "Who's Clara?" hand perceptive players the answer too early. The §2 fixes to the frost and to Ellis's line pull it back to "wonder".
- **II M6 pool.** "Cal notices. Says nothing. He takes twenty back off the pile." doesn't say what the twenty is for. → "…and folds it into Ellis's shirt pocket without looking at him."
- **III M4.** "**ELLIS** *(on the phone, when Cal calls Roy's):* How much?" lands mid-scene as a jump. Mark it as a cut.
- **I M8.** "It will be in the aftermath." is unclear. → "The word outlives the band."

## 8. The single most important fix

**Stop Chapter IV from spending the midpoint early.** In IV M6, Cal's storeroom-window shot shows Ellis alone on the step and then, after the switch, Clara beside him. In IV M8, Cal's camera looks up at "an empty mezzanine". In IV M4, Riley watches Ellis walk toward the ice machine where Clara sits. Each one is the objective-camera reveal the whole game is built toward at V M16, and each one gets there before V M7, the bible's one sanctioned exception. Apply the three exact replacements in §2 (P1-1, P1-2, and the M4 blocking note), and update the design summary and `05-clara-grace-clue-timeline.md` to match.
