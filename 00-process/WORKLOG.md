## Map (user-supplied, 2026-09-28)

Three playable places, one continuous map, west to east:
- **Laurel City** (west): big city, loose Atlanta analog. Grid downtown, expressways (SR 400 Expressway, SR 9 Spur), big reservoir/lake system, rail line. Rock clubs, AOR radio, Southern Star Records, the Coliseum, Riley's parents' suburb.
- **Hollow Ridge** (center): small railroad/creek town, pop ~2,100. Main Street (pink segment), creek running north–south with a falls to the north, rail line south of town. US 19 west through the **Laurel Gap corridor**; road east through the **Tanner Valley corridor**. **North highlands (Stony Knob)** above town; **South Fork highlands** below.
- **Tannersville** (east): big city, loose Nashville analog. River bend, big rail yard, SR 60 Expressway. The Row (music publishing/session district), Tannersville College, Vale Music, the Holloways' suburb, the Mercer shop, the old Tabernacle (Ryman analog, empty since the radio show moved out in 1974).
- Everything else is **off-map**: regional tour towns, New York City, the national tour, the festival in upstate New York. These are dedicated mission spaces (Guarma model). Leaving the map edge = "away." Returning = "home."
- Real Atlanta and Nashville are NOT named as separate places. Real national history (Nixon, Ford/WIN, Saigon, Carter, Bicentennial, real bands, magazines, TV) stays real (Mafia III model). The state remains Georgia.

## Core canon decisions (made during rebuild)

Timeline expanded to ~23 months so success is earned: **Thursday Oct 10, 1974 → Saturday Aug 28, 1976**, epilogue to 1977 and a 1996 frame.

Ages / dates:
- Ellis Blake b. Sept 14, 1956. 18 at start. Dies Aug 28, 1976, age 19, seventeen days before turning 20.
- Grace Clara Blake b. Nov 2, 1958. Died Thursday April 12, 1973, age 14. Ellis was 16.
- Clara (construct) appears ~20–22. Not Grace's would-be age: she is the older sister Ellis needed, built from Grace's face and their mother's.
- Riley (Margaret Anne Riley; everyone calls her Riley; her mother calls her Maggie) b. 1955, 19, sophomore at Tannersville College, from Laurel City suburbs.
- Cal Mercer b. 1953, 21. Tannersville.
- Dean Holloway b. 1954, 20. Tannersville (wealthy suburb).
- Wayne Blake b. 1929, 45. L&N-style railroad section foreman on the line between the two cities.
- Lorraine (mother) left in 1965 (Ellis 9, Grace 6). Lives in Dayton, Ohio, remarried (Hubbard). Came to Grace's funeral, left same day.

The crash (full truth, revealed in layers):
- Thu Apr 12, 1973, ~7:40 pm, rain, **Tolliver Bend** on the South Fork road. Ellis (16, license six months) driving Wayne's 1968 Impala. Wayne working late on a derailment cleanup. Ellis had to fetch Grace from the Tolliver farm (she rode their old mare, Dolly) before his FIRST gig: Marlon's Thursday picking night, 8:30. She made him late; she wanted to come watch; he said no; argument escalated to leaving. Grace: "You're gonna leave. Just like her." Ellis: "When I go, I'm not coming back for you." Oncoming pickup crossed centerline on the bend; Ellis swerved right; right tires dropped off the unpaved edge; overcorrected; car rotated; passenger side into an oak. Grace pinned, conscious several minutes. Ellis: broken arm, concussion (explains amnesia). Witness Hollis Beam stopped, ran to a house to call, came back. Ellis: "I'm sorry. I'm not going anywhere. Stay with me." Grace: "Stop saying sorry." ... "Liar." ... "It's okay. Go on." She died before the ambulance (Pettigrew Funeral Home's combination car) arrived. Truck never identified. Deputy Lyle Pruitt worked the scene.
- Speed estimated at or near the limit. Report lists unidentified vehicle as possible contributing factor and "driver reported distraction."
- Wayne was told about the other truck and refused to believe it. He needed it to be somebody's fault.
- Ellis's "memory" of speeding was built from Wayne's accusations (memory as reconstruction).
- Ellis has avoided Tolliver Road for three years. The game quietly never lets him drive it until Ch VIII.

Clara:
- Began appearing ~late April 1974, around the first anniversary, the first Thursday Ellis played Marlon's (the gig Grace never saw).
- Named for grandmother **Clara Tate Blake** (1901–1970, took Ellis to Sacred Harp singings) and Grace's middle name. Raymond Tate (Clara Tate's brother, Wayne's uncle) was committed to **Milledgeville** (Central State Hospital).
- Wears a faded denim jacket with a small embroidered horse patch on the pocket: Grace's actual jacket (child size) hangs in Grace's closet (seen Ch IX).
- Resembles Grace at 14 AND Lorraine at 20 (the missing middle between two photographs, revealed Ch VIII).
- Function: the part of Ellis that couldn't leave Grace in the car. Clara = "Stay with me" given a body. Grace's actual last words were "Go on."

Clara rules (never break without intent):
1. Never affects the objective world. Anything she "does" physically is unverified or is Ellis's own action.
2. Only Ellis speaks with her. Others' lines are blocked so they plausibly address Ellis.
3. Never appears in objective shots, photographs, recordings. Objective wide shots are framed so her absence isn't noticed on first play.
4. The dog (Tater, Grace's dog) never reacts to her.
5. Never enters Grace's room until the end of Ch IX (deliberate break).
6. Never knows anything Ellis could not know, consciously or not.
7. Never goes to the cemetery.
8. Never stays long in a room with Wayne.
9. The switch camera never "catches" on her — until Ch VIII–IX, when it stutters on her and fails (a grammar violation that prefigures the failed switch after Ellis dies).
