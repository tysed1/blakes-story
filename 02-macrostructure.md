*Deliverable 2. Rebuilt from scratch after the V1 autopsy. The chapter files in `chapters/` are the detailed expression of this plan.*

Total main story ≈ 58 hours. With side content, 80–110.

**Proportions, by design.** Chapters I–IV (the ordinary life before fame) are 20 hours, a third of the game. The player has to live in 1974 long enough to miss it. Fame arrives in the second half, and the collapse arrives only after the player has spent forty hours wanting these four to make it.

**The mystery ladder** (the question the player is asking at each stage):
1. I–IV: *Who is Clara?* (Mostly not asked. She's just someone Ellis knows.) *What happened to the little girl in the photograph?*
2. V (end): *What is Clara?* (She isn't there.)
3. VI–VII: *Why Clara? Who is she to Ellis?* (Wayne goes pale at her description; the band doesn't see her.)
4. VIII: *Oh God. She's Grace, grown, with his mother's face.* (The photographs.) *Was it his fault?*
5. IX: *What did he say to her? What did she say back?*
6. X: *"Liar." "It's okay. Go on."*

**The crash ladder** (what the player knows):
- I: a car; a little girl; Wayne blames Ellis; a closed door.
- II: her name was Grace; she was his sister; he flinches at "sorry."
- III: Wayne buried a child.
- IV: "Grace died in a car wreck. I was there."
- V: the rain drive: a flash of a girl's hand on a dashboard. April 12 passes with a pie nobody eats and a ballgame.
- VI: Marlon tells Cal she was on her way to Ellis's first gig (Cal only).
- VIII: Tolliver Bend; "I was driving."
- IX: the report (a truck over the line; the date); the witness (she spoke); the argument ("I'm not coming back for you").
- X: her last words.

## CHAPTER I — BEFORE THE NOISE

**Dates:** Thursday Oct 10 – Saturday Oct 19, 1974. **Runtime:** 4.5 h. **Playable:** Ellis → Riley → Dean → Cal → all four.

**Thesis.** The player should finish Chapter I believing they have watched the accidental beginning of a rock band. They have actually met four people shortly before their lives became inseparable, and a family built around a room nobody enters.

**Primary movement.** Four strangers, each seen in their own world, collide at Marlon's on a Friday when the booked band doesn't show.

**World state.** Hollow Ridge in autumn: poor, proud, gossipy, trains every forty minutes. Nobody knows the band exists. Prices matter.

**Band state.** Nonexistent → one forty-five-minute set → a name on a chalkboard.

**Ellis/Clara.** Clara is a friend. Appears after the gig in the alley, rides home, refuses to come in. Appears in his room late at night. Rides to Stony Knob.
**Grace info.** A face-down 1964 portrait; a plastic horse; a door with a horse sticker at a child's height; Wayne's "You're real good at dealing with cars after the fact" and "You got to keep yours"; a headstone coupon book on the kitchen table.
**Ellis/Wayne.** Hostile, practiced, with one wordless kindness (a lunch wrapped in wax paper by Ellis's keys).
**Riley.** A philosophy student whose life is full without any of this: classes, Hannah, her mother on the phone, her guitar.
**Cal.** A working musician who fixes things and refuses to absorb other people's failures.
**Dean.** A rich kid from a loving family who can't stand the life that love has planned for him; cocaine is new and fun.

**Gameplay introduced.** Walking, interacting, driving (Ellis's '63 Valiant), the Room performance system, the money system (scarcity), the switch grammar, Observe (the notebook), garage work, a small chase.
**Music.** Ellis alone and nervous; then four strangers finding thirty seconds of lock; "No Name" given weight by Cal and a harmony by Riley.
**Major location.** Marlon's Tavern.
**Set piece.** The first set: a wrong chord followed by everyone, the first **turn**.
**Quietest scene.** Dawn: Wayne asleep at the kitchen table with a photograph under his hand; Ellis leaves a blanket folded beside him instead of putting it on him.
**Funniest scene.** Dean's party raid: dragging cymbal stands through a pasture with a stranger yelling "Drive."
**Most painful.** "Yeah. You got to keep yours."
**Ending transformation.** THE BLAKES written in a notebook, crossed out, not torn out.

**Missions**
- **Cold Open / 1. The Gig** (Ellis). Thursday at Marlon's, sixteen people. A Croce cover, a request, "No Name." Pay. The alley: Clara. The drive home (Clara: "Not that way," at the Tolliver Road turnoff). Wayne awake in the dark.
- **2. What Things Cost** (Ellis). Friday: breakfast (coupon book), Roy's garage, Wesley ("You got a band?"), a woman who can't afford a repair, the money system, the Tannersville College delivery. Ellis walks past a girl arguing under a tree → switch.
- **3. The Long Way Around** (Riley). Campus, a seminar that turns into a joke, the dorm, Hannah, her mother ("Maggie"), her guitar. A Chevelle takes the corner too fast → switch.
- **4. Good Family** (Dean). A cymbal stand, dinner in Belle Grove, a bedroom, his mother at the door, a party in the Tanner Valley, the best drumming anyone there has heard, blue lights, a stranger in the passenger seat ("Who are you?" "Drive."), a broken amplifier that has to be fixed tonight → Vale Music → switch.
- **5. Everything Has a Reason** (Cal). The workbench at midnight; Mr. Vale; the Mercer shop ("Still buzzing?"); Saturday in Gaslight Alley holding up a bad band; "Find fifteen." The job to fix Marlon's hum.
- **6. Four Strangers** (Cal → Riley → Dean). Friday Oct 18 at Marlon's. The booked band isn't coming. "This is nothing." / "Not yet."
- **7. Fifteen Minutes** (all). The back room: tempo, keys, "No Name," a harmony ("Again."), "Think in E minor," "If I go somewhere, stay with me."
- **8. One More Song** (all). The set; the turn; "It's called 'It Doesn't Have a Name'"; money; "Each."; the second verse; the chalkboard.
- **9. Home** (Ellis). The drive; the Starlite; Wayne awake; "Us?"; the closed door; Clara in his room; Stony Knob; Deputy Pruitt; dawn; the notebook.

## CHAPTER II — SECOND VERSE

**Dates:** Saturday Oct 19 – Saturday Nov 2, 1974. **Runtime:** 5 h. **Playable:** Riley → Dean → Ellis → all → Riley → Ellis.

**Thesis.** Finding each other was an accident. Staying together requires a decision, and each of them makes it for a different reason.

**Primary movement.** Dean decides they're a band and tells everyone individually. They rehearse in daylight and sound worse. They play a car lot. They sound better. They eat at a truck stop at four in the morning and take a picture.

**World state.** Three people in Tannersville and one in Hollow Ridge now think about each other. Marlon's has a second Friday.

**Band state.** A rehearsal schedule, four rules, stage signals ("home" is defined), the first song they wrote together ("Low Water"), a roadie who wandered in (Tully), a PA bought on credit.

**Ellis/Clara.** Appears after he smokes marijuana for the first time with Riley (nothing more, nothing less). Sits in a booth during rehearsal and says the new song is better. Visible in his mirror at 4:30 a.m.
**Grace info.** "Had a sister." "Grace." "You didn't do anything." The door open three inches; Wayne on her bed.
**Ellis/Wayne.** Half a tire. Twenty dollars a week rent. A Thursday night watching *The Waltons* in silence. "Night."
**Riley.** Realizes the band is changing how she writes; brings Ellis into her world; learns Grace's name.
**Cal.** Discovers they might be worth taking seriously; quietly rearranges money so Ellis can pay later.
**Dean.** Finds people who keep saying yes. First real friction with Cal: "Then stop acting like him."

**Gameplay.** Campus exploration; rehearsal as a playable arrangement puzzle; the car-lot gig (comedy performance); Tannersville as an open city; the first "Thursday" quiet mission with no objective but sitting.
**Music.** Worse in daylight, then better. "Low Water" from Dean's tapping.
**Major location.** Tannersville (campus, the Row, Belle Grove); Marlon's in daylight.
**Set piece.** Tannersville Motor Sales Autumn Savings Spectacular.
**Quietest.** Thursday: *The Waltons*; "Night." "Night."
**Funniest.** The inflatable escaping across the car lot while Cal keeps playing perfectly.
**Most painful.** "You didn't do anything." (She hasn't said sorry yet; he's answering something older.)
**Ending transformation.** A Polaroid on a mirror. "You think it'll last?" / "Do you want it to?" / "Then tomorrow, act like it."

**Missions**
- **1. Circulation** (Riley). Saturday work-study at the library desk; the song won't leave her head; Hannah hums it; Dean phones.
- **2. Four Calls** (Dean). The shoe on the founder's statue; Vale ("Ten."); Roy's garage (upside-down Ellis).
- **3. Half a Tire** (Ellis). The tire; Roy; "Need anything?" / "Didn't say money."; rent; Clara on the porch step.
- **4. Rules** (all). Sunday at Marlon's with the chairs up; rules; worse than Friday; "Kick with me"; the second verse opens; "home"; "Low Water"; Tully at the back door; Cal and Ellis on the back steps.
- **5. Thursday** (Ellis). The first Thursday without a gig.
- **6. Professional Musicians** (Cal → all). The car lot; the boy with the pick; Riley's homework; "Then stop acting like him"; the money.
- **7. The Long Way Home** (Riley → Ellis). Ellis on campus early; Landry's lecture on memory (Ellis in the back row); the quad; weed; "I don't want to die where I was born"; Grace; Clara in the grass.
- **8. Second Friday** (all). THE BLAKES on the sign; sixty people; "Low Water"; a napkin autograph; Martin Keller's card.
- **9. Four in the Morning** (all). The Starlite; Lynette; the four dollars; dreams; the Polaroid; "Don't lose us."
- **10. The Hallway** (Ellis). Grace's door open; Wayne; the mirror; Clara.

## CHAPTER III — SIGNAL

**Dates:** Nov 4 – Nov 29, 1974. **Runtime:** 5 h. **Playable:** Cal → Riley → Ellis → Dean → all.

**Thesis.** Until now The Blakes existed only where they were standing. This is the chapter their music leaves the room, and once something leaves you, you don't control what it becomes.

**Primary movement.** A recording at the college station, a 10-watt broadcast caught from a mountaintop, a show in a city that doesn't care, a farmhouse, mushrooms, a letter from a stranger.

**World state.** WTCR plays them. A few dozen strangers in Laurel City remember them. Thanksgiving.

**Band state.** A tape; an audience outside Marlon's; the first real fight about sobriety; the decision to "make something."

**Ellis/Clara.** Behind the studio glass beside Riley. Vivid and far away in a field on mushrooms ("I wish you'd come around more." "You sure?" "Okay."). Riley finds him talking to himself; plausible cover. In the van: "Riley couldn't see you." / "She's never met me." At the back of Marlon's during a show; Riley thinks he's smiling at someone behind her.
**Grace info.** "I already buried one child."
**Ellis/Wayne.** Wayne drives up Stony Knob in the dark to hear his son on the radio and says a deputy told him about it. Then the worst morning yet.
**Riley.** Discovers the studio; introduces Ellis to psychedelics without pushing; is told by Cal, "Neither do you" (know him).
**Cal.** Recording gear makes his ears an asset; hears the name "Clara" once and files it; loses his temper for the first time ("Fuck you, Dean").
**Dean.** Parties, plays brilliantly, calls Cal "Dad," and finds the sentence that reaches him.

**Gameplay.** Recording (overdubs, playback, takes); radio tuning; FM line-of-sight driving; first psychedelic sequence (subtle perception changes, no neon); night driving with a van full of intoxicated friends.
**Music.** The first recording; overdubbed harmony; Dean's unrestrained take.
**Major locations.** WTCR studio; Stony Knob; a Laurel City club; a South Fork farmhouse.
**Set piece.** "Dead Air": the radio relay from Roy's truck on Stony Knob to Riley's dorm floor to Dean's car at a red light to Cal's father's repair bench.
**Quietest.** Thanksgiving at the Starlite: Wayne and Ellis eating the $1.95 turkey plate and both getting excited, for about four minutes, about a rookie backup quarterback on the diner television.
**Funniest.** Dean screaming "THAT'S ME!" at a stranger at a red light.
**Most painful.** Dawn on the porch: "I already buried one child."
**Ending transformation.** Clara, in the Marlon's lot: "Don't forget me." Headlights wash across. Empty. Radio static; the song in rooms we don't see.

**Missions**
- **1. Tape** (Cal). Vale Music; a cassette of rehearsal; "People don't have parts." Riley brings an offer.
- **2. Borrowed Time** (Riley). Landry ("This isn't your best"); her mother; WTCR at night; Martin Keller; overdubs; Ellis in the dark booth; Clara behind the glass; playback.
- **3. Dead Air** (Ellis → relay). The garage radio; Roy; Stony Knob; the relay; "Guess you better write another one"; Wayne's truck at the pull-off with its dome light on; "Pruitt told me."
- **4. Out of Town** (Dean → Cal). Laurel City booking; Vale's van; US 19 through the Gap; the name argument; set democracy.
- **5. Strangers** (all). The Blind Tiger, Laurel City; indifference; a guitarist who thinks Riley is someone's girlfriend; a woman at the bar who stays.
- **6. The Farmhouse** (Dean → Cal → Riley → Ellis). The afterparty; an older bassist ("Friends make terrible bands"); mushrooms; "My daddy's uncle got sent to Milledgeville"; the field; Clara.
- **7. Night Road** (Cal → Ellis). The drive home; "You think if you're responsible enough nothing bad happens"; "Neither do you"; the back of the van; dawn; the porch.
- **8. Thanksgiving** (Ellis). The Starlite; the diner TV; Lynette.
- **9. Something Wrong** (Cal). Ellis at Vale; "Clara likes the old version." / "Who's Clara?"
- **10. Demand** (relay). The listener letter.
- **11. Signal** (all). Marlon's; the letter on the kick drum; "Let's make something"; the lot.

## CHAPTER IV — MOMENTUM

**Dates:** Dec 2 – Dec 21, 1974. **Runtime:** 5.5 h. **Playable:** Cal → Dean → Riley → Ellis → relay.

**Thesis.** There is a point where a dream stops being something you talk about and starts rearranging your life. Nothing dramatic happens. Everything changes.

**Primary movement.** Eight hours in a real studio; a private-press 45; a van; three towns in two nights; a motel room; a kiss; a manager; a Thursday in Laurel City.

**World state.** The 45 in a Tenth Street record store; a Laurel City club knows their name; WLSU plays them.

**Band state.** A record, a vehicle, a shared bank of jokes, Ellis and Riley, a promoter, six booked dates.

**Ellis/Clara.** At the motel ice machine ("You're scared of liking Riley"); behind Marlon's in daylight, sober (Marlon: "Who're you talking to?" / "Myself." / "Bad company."); under an EXIT sign at the Lantern; in the van (Cal hears whispering); "Take me with you." / "Promise."; in his bedroom when Wayne walks in, and Ellis is sober, and Wayne thinks he's on something, and we side with Ellis.
**Grace info.** "Grace died in a car wreck. I was there." Wayne: "She know about Grace?" / "You want me to tell her?"
**Ellis/Wayne.** Wayne weaponizes the secret; Ellis's terror is the tell. Wayne mentions Lorraine once ("Your mother used to say the same thing").
**Riley.** The first kiss. A paper left in a motel room. "You don't have to earn this."
**Cal.** Meets Theo Marchand at the Lantern. Writes "Clara?" in the ledger margin and crosses it out.
**Dean.** Steals the van keys. His father: "Are you high?" / "Good morning to you too."

**Gameplay.** Studio time as a resource; van shopping and haggling (Ellis's specialty); van customization; roadside repair; motel social sequence; the Lantern performance (first big room); city night open world in Laurel City.
**Music.** The tape-echo discovery; the embryonic sound; the cracked vocal kept.
**Major location.** Laurel City (studio, Tenth Street, the Lantern).
**Set piece.** The Lantern, Thursday Dec 19.
**Quietest.** Room 12 after the pillow fight: "She would've liked you."
**Funniest.** "SIX FEET?" / "I UNDERESTIMATED!" and the cow.
**Most painful.** Wayne, softly: "You want me to tell her?" and watching Ellis's face.
**Ending transformation.** A ledger margin: *Clara?* Crossed out once. Ellis under a car hearing his own record on a Laurel City college station.

**Missions**
- **1. Eight Hours** (Cal). Mockingbird Sound, Laurel City; the ledger; "Sounds dead." / "It's clean."; live in the room; the Echoplex; "You're writing around it"; the cracked vocal.
- **2. Five Hundred Copies** (Riley). The master to a pressing plant on the Row; $412 on credit; the sleeve; "It's ours" (the Polaroid stays private).
- **3. Four Hundred Dollars and a Terrible Idea** (Dean). Four vans; the possum; the Econoline; Ellis haggles; the keys; the cow.
- **4. Three Towns** (Cal → Riley → Ellis). A roadhouse in the Gap; the van overheats; a farmer with a hose; the Tanner Valley Motor Court; Room 12; the ice machine; a college show; a Laurel City return where a man knows their name; the kiss; "I owe somebody five dollars."
- **5. Monday** (relay). Riley's paper; Dean's father; Cal at Vale; Ellis's "band rate"; Wayne with the newspaper.
- **6. The Girl Nobody Knows** (Cal). "Who's she?"; "Clara liked it"; Riley: "Friend from Hollow Ridge?"; behind Marlon's in daylight.
- **7. Demand, Again** (Dean). Five hundred records in a box; Tenth Street; "I will not call." He calls. Eddie Farris phones Vale.
- **8. Thursday** (all). Everyone's life pushes back; "She know about Grace?"; the Lantern; Theo; Clara under the EXIT sign; the missed lyric covered; Eddie's six dates; Laurel City after midnight; "No." / "I KNEW IT."; the drive home.
- **9. Something Good** (Riley). A midnight call; a practice room; "I was there."; "You don't have to earn this."
- **10. The Wall** (Ellis). Posters and a Polaroid; a photo put back in a drawer; "Take me with you."; Wayne in the doorway.
- **11. Ledger** (Cal → Ellis). *Clara?* The radio under the car.

## CHAPTER V — VELOCITY

**Dates:** Dec 24, 1974 – May 2, 1975. **Runtime:** 6.5 h. **Playable:** Dean → Ellis → Cal → Riley → relay. **Contains the midpoint.**

**Thesis.** Momentum still feels controllable. Velocity doesn't. One yes creates the next yes before anyone has processed the last one.

**Primary movement.** Christmas in two houses; six dates and then twenty; a song about Clara; a fistfight; the fall of Saigon on a motel television; a record offer; a promise in a van; and a question Ellis can't answer.

**World state.** WLSU and then small stations across the Southeast play the 45; the Lantern sells out; a Tannersville record store puts them in the window; the Row still doesn't care.

**Band state.** A regional draw, a manager (Eddie), a label offer, a new song everyone agrees is their best, and a first physical fight.

**Ellis/Clara.** Hears her harmony on a radio broadcast where only Riley sings. Flickers younger for a frame. Grows possessive of his attention ("Nobody understands." / "She just did."). Says "El." Cannot say where they met. Cries. **Midpoint:** the camera pulls back and Ellis is alone under a streetlight holding air.
**Grace info.** The rain drive: a flash of a girl's hand on a dashboard. April 12 passes: a buttermilk pie from Ada Tolliver on the kitchen table that nobody eats, and a ballgame.
**Ellis/Wayne.** Christmas: Wayne fixed the heater in Ellis's car without saying so. Opening day of the Engineers' season (Saturday April 12, 1975): the best day they've had in two years, a story about Grace eating three hot dogs, "Your mother could sing that," and a drive home at forty miles an hour. Then Wayne walks into Marlon's for the first time since 1973.
**Riley.** Takes a B-minus on an exam after an all-night drive; misses another exam by choice; "own the cost."
**Cal.** Holds the tour together; punches Dean; "I know you're my friend."
**Dean.** Uses alone for the first time after his father's quiet "I know."

**Gameplay.** Condensed off-map touring (the van leaving the map edge); simultaneous two-vehicle mission; rain driving with subjective anxiety; the first interview (branching answers); a band fistfight that is ugly rather than heroic; the objective-camera midpoint.
**Music.** Radio play; the first sold-out headline show; "Still Here" written and debuted; the crowd goes silent for it.
**Major locations.** Belle Grove; off-map tour towns; Engineers Park; the Exit in Tannersville; Marlon's.
**Set piece.** Opening Day (Wayne and Ellis at the ballpark). And the midpoint.
**Quietest.** Wayne driving home at forty with both hands on the wheel, keeping to the right, after he saw his son flinch at headlights. Neither says anything.
**Funniest.** The first interview ("What do you call it?" / "Space death.").
**Most painful.** "Why are you doing this to me?" and Ellis apologizing to her, and the camera pulling back.
**Ending transformation.** The player knows Clara isn't there.

**Missions**
- **Cold open.** April: a crowd chanting a name. "Are they doing that for us?"
- **1. Christmas Eve** (Dean → Ellis). The Holloways' party; Patty; Richard is kind to Ellis and Dean hates it; Wayne and the heater; Grace's ornament; two tickets to opening day ("That's April." "Hm.").
- **2. Lettering** (Riley). The van gets its name.
- **3. Six Dates** (Cal). Off the map; a first autograph; "They knew it."
- **4. Dial** (Dean). The 45 on the van radio; Ellis hears a harmony nobody sang.
- **5. Where's Dean?** (Cal). Chattanooga; another band's dressing room; "a bank" / "an excuse"; food.
- **6. Two Cars** (Riley/Cal ‖ Ellis/Dean). An exam in the morning; rain; a flash; the hood of the van; "being forty"; B-minus.
- **7. The Kitchen Table** (Dean). "I don't want your life." / "Then I don't want to grow up." / "I know."
- **8. Still Here** (Ellis). Writing it; Clara flipping records; "Can I meet her?"; Riley's first anomaly.
- **9. Opening Day** (Ellis). April 12. The pie. The ballgame. The drive home.
- **10. Sold Out** (all). The Lantern; the first interview; "Still Here" premieres; Clara younger for a frame.
- **11. Choosing** (Riley). The Jacksonville support date against a Friday exam; she chooses.

## CHAPTER VI — THE OTHER SIDE OF THE GLASS

**Dates:** May 3 – Aug 16, 1975. **Runtime:** 6.5 h. **Playable:** Ellis → Dean → Cal → Riley → all.

**Thesis.** Four people make something none of them fully understands, in a room behind glass, while one of them is increasingly on the other side of a glass nobody else can see.

**Primary movement.** A contract saved by the one father nobody expected; a guitar a customer left for a transmission; a summer in a former funeral home; a second farmhouse; a confession in a field; a father's face going white.

**World state.** Summer 1975 (*Jaws*; Apollo–Soyuz). The band is regionally known and working on a record.

**Band state.** Signed; making *Borrowed Stone*; the sound is born; Riley knows about Clara; Cal knows where Grace was going.

**Ellis/Clara.** "Don't tell them." "Don't" as he signs. Through the farmhouse door, solid, on mushrooms, and then the **switch to Riley**: the room is empty and Ellis is talking to nobody. Clara flickers younger; crash fragments. Next morning, sober, she's still there: "You can see me." Beyond the studio glass, alone in the live room.
**Grace info.** Crash flashes (rain, a windshield, a siren). Raymond and Milledgeville confirmed. Wayne's face when Ellis describes Clara. Marlon tells Cal the gig.
**Ellis/Wayne.** "Was he dangerous?" / "No. Mostly scared." / "Are you hearing something?" / "No." Wayne comes to the studio and watches his son work. Then goes white and leaves. "WHO IS SHE?" / "You tell me."
**Riley.** Ends the spring semester without deciding; is told "I see her. All the time."; asks the practical, frightened questions; carries the secret.
**Cal.** Sunday mornings at Theo's; a structure-versus-chaos argument; learns about April 12, 1973 from Marlon and tells no one.
**Dean.** Watches his father tear a contract apart on the dining-room table on his behalf. Plays his best takes on speed at 2 a.m.

**Gameplay.** Contract reading (Richard's markup as a readable document); the Jazzmaster "glide" introduced; studio experimentation sandbox (microphone placement, tape reversal, room recording); the psychedelic switch reveal; a long field conversation with silence as a choice.
**Music.** *Borrowed Stone*: tape echo, the glide, room sound, "Shape Note" recorded in the dark.
**Major location.** Dalton Sound on the Row, Tannersville.
**Set piece.** "Pasture": the switch.
**Quietest.** Cal fixing Theo's reel-to-reel on a Sunday morning with the window open.
**Funniest.** Frank Dalton explaining that a former funeral home is the best-sounding room in Tannersville ("Very quiet clientele.").
**Most painful.** Wayne in the control room, listening to "Still Here," asking what Clara looks like.
**Ending transformation.** The album exists. From Riley's side of the glass, Ellis has his hand flat against it and the live room is empty.

**Missions**
- **1. Don't Tell Them** (Ellis). The morning after.
- **2. Fine Print** (Dean). Richard Holloway reads the contract. "Breakage. On vinyl."
- **3. Sign Here** (Cal). Southern Star, renegotiated. Clara behind Vance.
- **4. Transmission** (Ellis). Roy's Jazzmaster; the glide.
- **5. Two Worlds** (Riley). Landry: "What are you studying for?"; Hannah; the semester ends.
- **6. Dalton Sound** (all). The funeral home; "Don't touch anything you don't understand."; "There it is."
- **7. 2:13 A.M.** (Dean). "Again."; Cal asks for a cigarette.
- **8. Sunday Morning** (Cal). Theo's apartment. That night Riley sees them together at a club and smiles at her drink.
- **9. Pasture** (Riley → Ellis → Riley). The farmhouse; mushrooms; the door; the switch.
- **10. The Field** (Ellis). "I see her." "Does she tell you to hurt yourself?" "No doctors."
- **11. Afterimage** (Ellis). Sober morning; "It's the only answer that matters."
- **12. Bloodline** (Ellis). Raymond.
- **13. Side B** (Ellis → Cal). Frank and the room; "Shape Note" in the dark; "Do both."
- **14. Visitor** (Ellis). Wayne at Dalton Sound.
- **15. Marlon's PA** (Cal). April 12, 1973.
- **16. Final Playback** (all). "What if this is it?"; Vance: "not the record I thought I was buying"; *Borrowed Stone*.

## CHAPTER VII — STRANGERS KNOW YOUR NAME

**Dates:** Sept 8 – Dec 13, 1975. **Runtime:** 7 h. **Playable:** all four in rotation.

**Thesis.** Ownership. Ellis loses his story, Riley her name, Cal the band's public identity, Dean his hold on the drug. The record belongs to its listeners now. So do they.

**Primary movement.** Release; silence; a review; a station at 11:47 p.m.; sixteen hundred people singing Clara's words; a garage left behind; an article that decides who Ellis is; a Thanksgiving table with an extra chair; the first real band fight; New York; an envelope from Dayton, Ohio.

**World state.** WLRC in heavy rotation; posters on Tenth Street; teenagers in homemade shirts; the Row starts saying their name; Hollow Ridge splits between pride and annoyance; the map remembers them.

**Band state.** Professional. A crew (Tully; Theo on sound). A manager. A national writer. Money arriving, and Cal as the band's accountant. The first public fracture.

**Ellis/Clara.** In crowds, interviews and hotel halls. "Who's Clara?" from a fan; "Old friend" enters the mythology. The first cruel thing: "They don't know you. Neither does Riley." Ellis argues with her aloud in a hotel hallway; a fan overhears; a rumor starts.
**Grace info.** The cemetery (optional, unlocked): CLARA TATE BLAKE; GRACE CLARA BLAKE. Roy mentions Danny. A gas-station man: "Your sister would've liked it."
**Ellis/Wayne.** Wayne at a show: "Loud." / "I liked it." / "Would've hated Dean." Wayne asks, carefully, whether that girl is still around.
**Riley.** Doesn't register for fall semester; tells her parents; Joan: "I had a place at Oberlin in 1949." Hears "I love you" while holding a flashlight in the rain.
**Cal.** Ignored by fans; "It's ours" quoted inside the Ellis story; seen with Theo by Ellis, who says only "He seems all right."
**Dean.** Cut off by his father; fixes the ledger at 4 a.m. and swears Cal to secrecy; Lynette.

**Gameplay.** Open-world fame layer (recognition, posters, radio rotation); fan encounters; interviews as a dialogue system where the press rewrites your answers; money management shifts to Cal; the Four Rooms (no-proximity switching); off-map New York.
**Music.** *Borrowed Stone* released; "Still Here" breaks; bigger rooms; crowds sing.
**Major locations.** Tenth Street record store; WLRC; the Civic Auditorium; Linwood; a Richmond hotel; New York.
**Set piece.** Sixteen hundred people singing Clara's first words to Ellis back to him.
**Quietest.** Ellis and Joan Riley at the piano after Thanksgiving dinner, singing a shape-note tune his grandmother taught him while Riley watches her mother light up.
**Funniest.** The surprise nineteenth birthday party Ellis did not want at Marlon's.
**Most painful.** "Pain doesn't automatically make everything you do important."
**Ending transformation.** A famous photograph of four young musicians, Ellis's face unreadable, an unopened envelope from Dayton in his jacket pocket.

**Missions**
- **1. Nineteen** (Ellis). Sept 14; the party he didn't want; Riley's leather notebook.
- **2. Release Day** (all). Tenth Street; a stranger buys it.
- **3. Occasionally Astonishing** (Riley). Reviews.
- **4. Registration** (Riley). Linwood; Oberlin, 1949.
- **5. 11:47 P.M.** (Cal → relay). Martin Keller, now on WLRC.
- **6. Last Shift** (Ellis). Roy; Danny; "Keep the shirt."
- **7. Where's Ellis?** (Cal).
- **8. Sixteen Hundred** (all). The Civic Auditorium; "Are they doing that for us?"; "Who's Clara?"
- **9. East Slope** (Ellis, optional). The cemetery.
- **10. The Extra Chair** (Riley). Thanksgiving in Linwood.
- **11. Hold the Light** (Ellis → Riley). Riley's Datsun on US 19 in the rain; "I love you, hold it still."
- **12. Who the Hell Is Ellis Blake?** (all). Dex Lundgren.
- **13. Ledger** (Dean → Cal). 4 a.m. arithmetic.
- **14. Belle Grove** (Dean). Cut off; the Starlite; Lynette.
- **15. Loud** (Ellis). Wayne at the Laurel City show.
- **16. Room 614** (Ellis). Richmond; Cal and Theo in a hallway.
- **17. Twenty-One Dates** (Cal). The fight.
- **18. Four Rooms** (relay).
- **19. North** (all). Off the map; Virginia radio; New York; *Deliverance* jokes; a Bowery punk who calls them hippies; the showcase; an envelope; the photograph.

## CHAPTER VIII — FEEDBACK

**Dates:** Jan 12 – Apr 30, 1976. **Runtime:** 6.5 h. **Playable:** Cal → Riley → Dean → Ellis → Riley → Ellis. **This chapter did not exist in the original draft.**

**Thesis.** Feedback is a signal re-entering itself until it screams. The band built its sound on controlling it. In this chapter the loop runs through the industry, the press, the drugs and Ellis's mind, and nobody can find the volume.

**Primary movement.** A major label and a key-man clause; Riley's song sung by Ellis for the radio; an arena tour in winter; a critic on the bus; an irregular heartbeat in Cleveland; a hotel-bar kiss printed in a magazine; the cruelest thing Ellis ever says; forty seconds of silence on national television; a box in a closet; Tolliver Road; a psychiatrist.

**World state.** "Still Here" in the Top 40; "Sunday Clothes" climbing; the NY photograph on dorm walls; national press; Carter winning primaries; *Frampton Comes Alive!* everywhere; CB radios; *One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest* in theaters.

**Band state.** National. Separate hotel rooms. A tour bus. A product manager who calls Ellis "the franchise." Cal leaves for two shows. The band's promise is broken in every clause.

**Ellis/Clara.** Intrusive: in interviews, on the bus, on a soundstage between cameras. The switch camera **catches on her, stutters, and fails** (twice). "He's taking me" (about Dex). After the photographs: Grace at fourteen in the passenger seat at Tolliver Bend, aging into Clara; "I made you." / "Does that make me less real?" Then trifluoperazine, and silence.
**Grace info.** Wayne tells Riley that Clara was his mother's name and Grace's middle name and that Grace sang harmony. The photographs: Grace at thirteen, Lorraine at nineteen, Clara between them. "I was driving."
**Ellis/Wayne.** "There is no Clara." / "She's right there." Wayne drives Ellis to Laurel City to Dr. Lusk and waits in the truck for two hours.
**Riley.** "Sunday Clothes" sung by Ellis; her mother hears it on the radio; Nina Sorensen writes about her; a trip turns on her and she stops; reads about the kiss in a magazine; drives to Wayne.
**Cal.** Signs the key-man clause under protest; counts Clara conversations ("Eleven times this week"); is outed by Ellis to Dean in front of the crew; leaves; comes back without an apology.
**Dean.** In paradise on an arena tour; chest pain and an irregular heartbeat in a Cleveland ER; Lynette hangs up on him. Learns about Cal and handles it like Dean.

**Gameplay.** Arena-scale performance (bigger room, slower crowd response); tour bus as social hub; the grammar-violating switch; the television performance where the player's only input is to continue; searching the Blake house; the first drive down Tolliver Road; the psychiatrist's office as a dialogue scene with silence; the medicated free-roam where audio and color flatten.
**Music.** Singles; arena sound; a song ("New Skin") begun and abandoned.
**Major locations.** Monarch's New York office; winter Midwest arenas; a Cleveland ER; *Night Stage* soundstage; Cold Branch Road; Tolliver Bend.
**Set piece.** *Night Stage*: forty seconds of live-to-tape silence that the producers keep because it's electrifying.
**Quietest.** Ellis on the porch on medication, waiting for someone who doesn't come, Tater's head on his boot.
**Funniest.** Dean learning about Cal and, a day later, bringing him coffee: "For the record, I figured you were just boring."
**Most painful.** Ellis to Cal: "At least mine isn't somebody I keep in a hotel room."
**Ending transformation.** The notebook open on his knee. The pen doesn't move.

**Missions**
- **Cold open.** A New York soundstage in March; a red light; Ellis stops.
- **1. Key Man** (Cal). Monarch Records; Gil Tarver; the clause.
- **2. Single Version** (Riley). "Sunday Clothes" re-sung by Ellis.
- **3. The Opener** (Dean). Winter arenas.
- **4. The Bus** (Ellis). Dex Lundgren rides along; a page goes missing.
- **5. Cleveland** (Dean → Cal). The ER.
- **6. Hotel Bar** (Ellis). "I know who Clara is."
- **7. Eleven Times** (Cal). The fight.
- **8. No Floor** (Ellis). Two shows without Cal.
- **9. Kitchen, Cold Branch Road** (Riley). Wayne and Riley.
- **10. Night Stage** (Ellis). The silence.
- **11. Rave** (Riley). The magazine.
- **12. The Box in the Closet** (Ellis). The photographs; "There is no Clara."
- **13. Tolliver Road** (Ellis). The bend; the oak; "I made you."
- **14. Payphone** (Riley). "I was driving."
- **15. Four Chairs** (Riley → Ellis). The kitchen; the decision.
- **16. Dr. Lusk** (Ellis). "I don't know yet what this is."
- **17. Quiet** (Ellis). The porch; the pen.

## CHAPTER IX — WHO ARE YOU?

**Dates:** May 10 – Aug 22, 1976. **Runtime:** 6 h. **Playable:** Cal → Ellis → Dean → Riley → Ellis.

**Thesis.** The world discovers Ellis the poet at the exact moment Ellis discovers what he said in the car. Everyone is asked who they are. Grace, on a tape made when she was thirteen, has already answered for him.

**Primary movement.** A lodge on Stony Knob with a recording truck in the drive; medication that makes him quiet and famous; a legendary interview; the accident report; a witness in North Carolina; the argument remembered; pills flushed; Dean's bottom; the Tabernacle; Riley leaving the lodge; poems in a magazine; an apology at a radio bench; Grace's room; the tapes; a fourteen-year-old in a hallway.

**World state.** Bicentennial summer. Carter nominated. The poems in print. Fans theorizing that Clara is a dead sister. A publisher. Hollow Ridge sick of tourists and proud of them.

**Band state.** Recording the second album at home. Healing and splitting at once. Dean sober. Riley out of the lodge. Cal and Ellis mended.

**Ellis/Clara.** Gone for weeks (medication). The switch catches on an empty chair and fails. Returns on day four after he stops, fiercer, brighter. At the Tabernacle, radiant. In Grace's hallway, fourteen: "I couldn't be her." She walks into Grace's room, the first time. He closes the door.
**Grace info.** The report (April 12, 1973, the player remembers a pie and a ballgame); the truck over the line; Wayne knew. Hollis Beam: "She told you to stop saying sorry." The argument: "I'm not coming back for you." The tapes: WGRC; harmony; "Who are you?" / "You're my brother." / "That's not enough." / "It is to me." A playable afternoon in 1972 with Grace alive.
**Ellis/Wayne.** "I needed it to be somebody's fault." Boiled peanuts on a tailgate in North Carolina. "You want me there?" / "Yeah."
**Riley.** Stops using psychedelics; finds the untouched pill count; "I can't watch you choose her"; moves to Laurel City; stays in the band.
**Cal.** Tells Ellis about his grandfather and the radio. Accepts an apology delivered as a soldering job.
**Dean.** Flushes his stash; "I think I need help"; three nights with Cal reading *The Dispossessed* aloud badly; goes to Lynette sober.

**Gameplay.** The lodge as a small open hub (dock, lake, truck, kitchen, bedrooms); the medicated Ellis (slower movement, muted Observe, no Clara); the *Late Hour* interview (timing-based silence system); document reading (the report); off-map North Carolina; the Tabernacle performance with a Sacred Harp class onstage (the hollow square); playing a memory as sixteen-year-old Ellis with Grace.
**Music.** "Who Are You?" begun; "New Skin" finished; the Tabernacle show.
**Major locations.** Stony Knob lodge; *Late Hour* studio; Sylva, North Carolina; the Tabernacle; Mercer Radio & TV; Grace's room.
**Set piece.** The Tabernacle: a Sacred Harp class in a hollow square on the stage of a building the country establishment abandoned, singing with a rock band.
**Quietest.** Wayne and Ellis on a tailgate eating boiled peanuts outside Sylva. "Grace hated these." "She ate them anyway."
**Funniest.** Dean's detox, night two: Cal reading Le Guin aloud in a monotone and Dean begging him to do the voices.
**Most painful.** Clara, fourteen, in the hallway: "I couldn't be her."
**Ending transformation.** A closed door. A list on a notebook page. "Dad — Sunday?"

**Missions**
- **1. The Lodge** (Cal). Moving in; the truck; the dock; Frank.
- **2. Under Glass** (Ellis). Medicated; fishing with Cal; the grandfather and the radio.
- **3. Late Hour** (Ellis). New York, June.
- **4. The Report** (Ellis). Pruitt's file; Wayne's confession.
- **5. Sylva** (Ellis). Hollis Beam; peanuts.
- **6. The Argument** (Ellis → Riley). The memory; "Words don't steer trucks."
- **7. Flush** (Ellis). Day one to day four; Clara returns; "Who Are You?"
- **8. Bottom** (Dean → Cal). "I think I need help."
- **9. The Tabernacle** (all). The hollow square.
- **10. Count** (Riley). The pills; the lie; leaving the lodge.
- **11. Excerpts** (Ellis). The poems in *Rave*.
- **12. Bench** (Ellis → Cal). Mercer Radio & TV.

## CHAPTER X — THE LAST LIGHT

**Dates:** Thursday Aug 26 – Saturday Aug 28, 1976. **Runtime:** 4 h. **Playable:** Cal → Dean → Riley → Ellis → all → Riley → Cal → Dean → Wayne.

**Thesis.** Everything the game taught (switching, following, turns, home, stay with me, the crowd, the light, the road) converges in one song. Ellis remembers his sister telling him to go, turns toward the living, and a light he doesn't see coming does the rest.

**Primary movement.** A bus north with two old men in a pickup behind it; a racetrack in upstate New York; a tab of acid from a notebook; an interview that will be called prophecy; a walk through 150,000 people; a squall and a three-hour delay; a green room; a promise; a walk to the stage; the sunset set; the last light.

**World state.** The largest crowd of their lives; a concert film crew; helicopters; mud from a hurricane two weeks gone; a state permit fight; Carter on buttons.

**Band state.** At their best and least safe. Dean sober. Riley and Ellis tender and not together. Cal watching everything.

**Ellis/Clara.** At his side on the walk: "The part that couldn't leave her there." At the stage edge with Grace. When he remembers, there is nothing left for her to hold.
**Grace info.** Complete: "Stop saying sorry." "Liar." "It's okay. Go on."
**Ellis/Wayne.** Wayne came. Ellis sees him in the crowd during the set.
**Riley.** Reaches for him with her hand out.
**Cal.** Asks what day it is and takes the answer as evidence.
**Dean.** The man who said yes to everything: "We can cancel."

**Gameplay.** Festival crowd traversal; LSD perception (subtle, beautiful, then not); the green-room decision; the longest backstage walk in the game; the final performance with rapid switching; the "home" cue as the only available input; the fall; the failed switch; hospital; the first playable Wayne.
**Music.** "Who Are You?" with an improvised last verse.
**Major location.** The Arbor Jam, Glen Arbor Raceway, New York.
**Set piece.** The Last Light.
**Quietest.** Ellis in the field at noon under a hat, unrecognized, writing overheard sentences on his forearm.
**Funniest.** The green room before it goes wrong: Dean producing the Polaroid ("We look terrible." / "You look terrible."), the dealership remembered.
**Most painful.** The crowd chanting his name while he says he isn't him.
**Ending transformation.** The camera tries to find Ellis and can't.

**Missions**
- **1. North** (Cal). The bus; the radio; the F-100 in the mirror.
- **2. Glen Arbor** (Dean → Riley). The site; the film crew; Theo on the tower; Wayne and Roy park in a hayfield.
- **3. Morning** (Ellis). The motel; the notebook; the tab.
- **4. Press Tent** (Ellis). "Like standing at the edge of something."
- **5. The Field** (Ellis). Walking the crowd; Grace.
- **6. Weather** (Cal). The squall; the delay; the green room; "One show. Then we go home." / "Promise?"
- **7. The Walk** (Ellis). Clara.
- **8. The Last Light** (all). The set.
- **9. The Gap** (Riley → Cal → Dean → Wayne).
