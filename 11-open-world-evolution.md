# THE BLAKES — Open-World Evolution

*Deliverable 13. How the one continuous map (Laurel City, Hollow Ridge, Tannersville, and the country between) changes across twenty-three months, so that the world itself tells the story. The rule: the player should be able to tell what chapter it is by walking down Main Street with the HUD off.*

## The map, in brief

- **Laurel City** (west): the rock city. Tenth Street, the Lantern, Southern Star, WLRC 97.1, the Civic Auditorium, Engineers Park, Linwood, the university medical district, the SR 400 Expressway and the SR 9 Spur.
- **The Laurel Gap corridor** (US 19): the Starlite, the Pine Knot trailer court, peanut stands, pulpwood trucks.
- **Hollow Ridge** (center): Main Street, Marlon's, Roy's, the depot and the rail line, the Methodist church and cemetery, the monument yard, Cold Branch Road and the Blake house, Stony Knob (the overlook, the fire tower, the pull-off, and from IX, Knob House), the South Fork (Tolliver Road, the farm, the bend, Pickens Grocery, the farmhouse, the low-water bridge).
- **The Tanner Valley corridor**: the Tanner Valley Motor Court (Room 12, the ice machine).
- **Tannersville** (east): the Row (Dalton Sound in the old Pearce & Sons funeral home, the publishers), the Tabernacle, Tannersville College and WTCR, Vale Music, Mercer Radio & TV by the rail yard, Belle Grove, Cutler Street, Gaslight Alley, the Exit.
- **Off-map mission spaces**: tour towns (V, VII), Richmond (VII), New York (VII, VIII, IX), the winter Midwest arenas (VIII), Sylva, North Carolina (IX), Glen Arbor Raceway (X), Route 17 home (Epilogue).

## The radio as a world system

The in-game radio is the open world's memory. It changes with the story:
- **I–III:** AM country and Top 40; FM only in Laurel City, on converters, and on Stony Knob.
- **III:** WTCR's broadcast carries the camera from radio to radio (a relay).
- **VII M6 onward:** "Still Here" in WLRC rotation. The player can hear it from taxis, apartment windows, the Starlite kitchen.
- **VIII:** "Sunday Clothes" (Ellis's single; Riley's harmony per the player's choice); "Convoy"; Frampton.
- **IX:** Charlene Hobbs's countrypolitan "Sunday Clothes"; the Bicentennial.
- **X:** "Don't Go Breaking My Heart" everywhere; the festival live on WLRC.
- **Ep.:** every station playing the Blakes, including a bootleg of the last verse, which Cal turns off; talk radio arguing about drugs; the chart climbing to #1.

## The fame layer (from VII)

- **Recognition.** NPC reactions scale by city and chapter: Laurel City first (VII), Tannersville later (VIII–IX), Hollow Ridge divided throughout. Recognition is never a meter; it's who looks twice.
- **Stage doors.** Crowds after shows (VII M8, VIII); the player can sign, talk, or leave.
- **Mail.** Fan letters arrive at Southern Star and are forwarded (Kevin from the peanut stand; a prisoner in Alabama; Patty; Lorraine).
- **Press.** *Rave*; *Metro*; *Night Stage*; *Late Hour*; the press tent. Every appearance runs through the interview system, and the press selects from what the player said.
- **Money.** Scarcity (I–IV) → enough (V–VII) → too much (VIII–X), which the UI shows less and less.

## After the death

The world keeps moving without him, and the game lets the player watch it:
- The radio never stops playing the Blakes, and the player can't find a station that isn't.
- The fans come to Hollow Ridge, and the town is kind to them and tired of them.
- The switch has no one to go to. From the epilogue on, missions start where they start, with no carry.
- **The coda restores the first world entirely**: the town-limit sign clean, the chalkboard saying FRIDAY — THE BLAKES, Ellis turning around at the Tolliver Road turnoff, Tater enormous, no fame, no Clara, and one objective at dusk.
